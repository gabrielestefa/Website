About

Where art
meets engineering

I'm an Environment & Technical Artist graduating from Howest DAE — one of the world's top-rated game art programs — with a specialisation in game graphic production and a technical minor.

My strength is in finding the workflow that works. Whether that's a Houdini HDA that scatters biomes intelligently, a Python pipeline that automates bake submission, or a Blueprint assembly system for modular rooftop kits — I build the tools that let the art happen faster and better.

I've shipped work for Dread XP and produced an Unreal Marketplace asset pack for Voyager 3D, handling the full pipeline solo. Currently open to internship and junior roles.

Based in Italy — available internationally
